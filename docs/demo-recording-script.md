# Nalbace demo recording script

Target 2 minutes 30 seconds. Start on Today in a phone-sized browser window. Use saved test evidence. Keep operator screens and inboxes out of frame. Review the exported recording for contact details and private information before uploading.

## 0:00–0:30 | Today

“When you run a small business, it’s easy for an important customer reply to get lost between invoices, calls, and email. Nalbace brings those accounts into a daily review queue. Here’s a dispute at the top. I can see why it needs attention without opening every invoice.”

## 0:30–1:00 | Messages

“This test customer replied to an approved reminder. Nalbace matched the reply to the invoice and put follow-up on hold. I can review the customer’s words and record my decision. I’ll use a synthetic example for the public recording and keep the private test message off-screen.”

Recording note: If the only available reply includes personal details or profanity, show the queue and decision controls with the message and identity covered in the edit. Keep enough of the screen visible to demonstrate the interaction.

## 1:00–1:25 | Phone dispute

“Here’s a saved result from a separate, consented AI test call. The customer said they disputed the invoice. Nalbace saved their words and brought the case back to the owner to review.”

## 1:25–2:00 | PayPal payment evidence

“In this PayPal sandbox test, the customer paid a $25 invoice. Nalbace checked the payment evidence and showed a zero balance. It blocked further reminders. An open relationship concern can still need attention after the invoice is settled.”

## 2:00–2:30 | Settings and close

“In Settings, I can choose when an overdue invoice becomes urgent. Disputes and unresolved relationship concerns stay visible regardless of that threshold. Nalbace gives a busy owner the context to decide how to follow up and when a customer needs their personal attention.”
